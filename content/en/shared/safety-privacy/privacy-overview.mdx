ConnectPi values pioneer privacy and adheres to minimal data collection principles.

## What information is collected
- Public username and display handle.
- Authenticated Pi Network UID (hashed for authentication).
- Voluntarily posted public discussions and community reactions.

<Callout type="note" title="Zero Passphrase Storage">
ConnectPi operates entirely non-custodially. We never request, process, or store your 24-word cryptographic seed passphrase.
</Callout>

## Controlling your profile privacy
You can control who views your active status, loyalty leaderboard position, and notifications directly from your profile settings menu.
