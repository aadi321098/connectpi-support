ConnectPi utilizes official **Pi Network Authentication** so Pioneers can seamlessly create an account and sign in without remembering separate passwords. Your account is securely linked to your verified Pi identity.

There are two primary methods to sign in depending on whether you are using the mobile **Pi Browser** or an external browser.

---

## Method 1: Inside Pi Browser (Recommended for Mobile)

Signing in through the official Pi Browser provides instant, one-tap authentication verified directly by the Pi ecosystem.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pi Browser app">
    Launch the official **Pi Browser** on your smartphone.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to ConnectPi">
    In the URL address bar at the top of the Pi Browser, enter either of the following addresses:
    - `https://connectpi.in/signup`
    - `https://aditya.pinet.com/signup`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click 'Continue with Pi'">
    On the **Join or Login to Connect Pi** welcome screen, locate the **PI BROWSER** section at the top and tap the blue **Continue with Pi** button.

    <Screenshot
      src="/images/articles/web/pi-browser-login-choice.webp"
      alt="ConnectPi sign in screen with Continue with Pi button highlighted"
      caption="Tap 'Continue with Pi' in the Pi Browser section to begin instant authentication."
    />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grant Pi account permissions">
    An official Pi Network dialog titled **'Share information with Connect Pi ?'** will appear. ConnectPi requests the following standard permissions:
    - **Auth:** Authenticates you on ConnectPi with your verified Pi identity.
    - **Username:** Synchronizes your public Pi username.
    - **Wallet address:** Connects your public wallet address for community tipping and reward distributions.
    - **In app notifications:** Allows the app to notify you about likes, comments, and community updates.

    Tap the purple **Allow** button to confirm.

    <Screenshot
      src="/images/articles/web/pi-auth-modal-allow.webp"
      alt="Pi Network authorization prompt with purple Allow button highlighted"
      caption="Tap the purple 'Allow' button to securely grant access and complete sign in."
    />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Welcome to ConnectPi!">
    Your account is instantly verified and created (or logged in). You will be redirected straight to your ConnectPi community feed where you can start engaging with fellow Pioneers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Security Reminder: Never Share Your Passphrase">
  Official Pi authentication occurs entirely through the automated Pi SDK dialog shown above. ConnectPi will **NEVER** ask you to enter, paste, or share your **24-word Pi wallet passphrase**. If any website asks for your passphrase, close it immediately.
</Callout>

## Method 2: Outside Pi Browser (Desktop, Chrome, or Any Standard Browser)

If you are accessing ConnectPi from a laptop, desktop computer, or a standard mobile browser (such as Google Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Brave):

1. Visit [connectpi.in/signup](https://connectpi.in/signup).
2. Locate the **DESKTOP / CHROME / ANY BROWSER** card.
3. Click the **Sign in with Pi Account** button.

<Callout type="info" title="Desktop Authentication Guide">
  External browser authentication links your desktop session to your verified Pi identity. Full step-by-step instructions for desktop and external browser sign-in are detailed in the next section.
</Callout>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my display name after signing in?
Yes. Your Pi username is permanently attached as your unique Pioneer identifier, but you can set and update your custom profile display name, bio, and avatar anytime under **Profile Settings**.

### What happens if I click 'Decline'?
If you select *Decline* on the Pi permissions modal, ConnectPi cannot verify your Pi account, and you will not be able to log in. You can simply tap *Continue with Pi* again and choose *Allow* whenever you are ready.
