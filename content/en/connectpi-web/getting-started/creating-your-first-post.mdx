ConnectPi is the social home for the global Pi Network community. Creating posts lets you share updates, trade ideas, discuss Pioneer initiatives, and earn engagement rewards.

---

## 1. Opening the Post Creator

You can start creating a post from several convenient locations across the platform:

### On Desktop & Laptops
When browsing [connectpi.in](https://connectpi.in) on your computer, you have two instant entry points:
- **Option A (Sidebar):** Click the purple **+ New Post** button in the left navigation sidebar.
- **Option B (Home Banner):** Click the white **Create Post** button (or the *"What's happening in Pi Network...?"* prompt) in the central home feed banner.

### On Mobile Devices & Pi Browser
When accessing ConnectPi on your mobile phone:
- **Option A (Home Banner):** Tap the **Create Post** button in the top banner.
- **Option B (Floating Action Button):** Tap the round blue **(+) Floating Action Button (FAB)** fixed at the bottom right corner of your screen.

## 2. Composing Your Post & Using Media Tools

Clicking any of the post buttons opens the **Create a new post** modal editor:

<Screenshot
  src="/images/articles/web/create-post-modal-tools.webp"
  alt="Create a new post dialog showing text editor, audience visibility pills, media buttons, and the Post button"
  caption="The ConnectPi post editor equipped with media attachments, mentions, and audience filters."
/>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your message & check character limits">
    Type your message into the **"What's happening...?"** text area.
    - **Standard Accounts:** Up to **350 characters**.
    - **Premium Accounts:** Up to **700 characters** with an exclusive Pioneer badge.
    
    A live character counter in the bottom-right corner displays your remaining allowance.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Attach photos, videos, or paste from clipboard">
    You can enrich your post with media through three flexible methods:
    - **File Picker:** Click the **Gallery (Image)** icon or **Camera (Video)** icon in the bottom toolbar to upload media files from your device.
    - **Drag & Drop:** Drag an image or video directly from your computer files and drop it onto the dialog.
    - **Clipboard Paste:** Copy any screenshot (`Ctrl+C` or `Cmd+C`) and press `Ctrl+V` inside the editor to upload instantly!
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tag Pioneers with Mentions (@) and Hashtags (#)">
    - Click the **@** icon (or type `@` in your text) to search for any registered Pioneer by username and tag them with instant autocomplete.
    - Use `#hashtags` (e.g. `#PiNetwork`, `#ConnectPi`, `#Crypto`) to group your post with trending discussions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your audience visibility">
    Under **"Who can see this post?"**, select your target distribution:
    - **ALL:** Broadcasted across all public feeds.
    - **Global:** Featured in international discovery feeds.
    - **Local:** Targeted to Pioneers in your geographic region (requires enabling *Share Location*).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a topic category">
    Click **Choose** under the category selector to tag your post (e.g. *General*, *Pi Network*, *Tech*, *Marketplace*, or *Governance*). Categorizing posts helps other Pioneers discover your content.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish your post">
    Review your post and tap the blue **Post** button in the bottom right corner. Your content is published live immediately!
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

<Callout type="warning" title="Important Notice: All Posts are Public">
  Once you click **Post**, your content is published to the public ConnectPi decentralized social network. Any Pioneer worldwide can view your post, interact with comments, or download public media attachments.
  
  Please ensure your content adheres to **ConnectPi Community Guidelines**. Never share personal financial keys, passwords, or confidential private data in public posts.
</Callout>
