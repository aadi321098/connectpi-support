## 3. Fruit Slice Masterclass (Blade Slashing Mechanics)

**Fruit Slice** is the flagship action title within Rush Box. 

### Game Rules
- **Three Hearts System:** Every round begins with 3 hearts. Missing normal fruits or slicing red hazardous bombs deducts hearts.
- **Combo Multipliers:** Slice 3 or more fruits in a single fluid swipe to trigger a **Combo Multiplier** (`3x`, `4x`, `5x+`), drastically increasing your point total.
- **Golden Dragonfruit Frenzy:** Slicing special golden fruits triggers a **10-second Frenzy Mode**, flooding the screen with fruits while bomb spawns are paused!

### Active Power-Ups
Players can deploy power-ups to protect streaks and maximize scores:

- **Shield:** Negates damage from the next bomb sliced.
- **Freeze:** Slows object gravity and flight trajectories, making precision multi-fruit swipes effortless.
- **Heart Shield:** Grants an additional reserve heart to recover from near-fatal mistakes.

## 4. RB Coins, Daily Tasks & Progression

| Reward Mechanism | Requirement | RB Coins Payout |
|---|---|:---:|
| **Level Progression** | Clear levels across Level Mode chapters | **+5 to +25 RB** |
| **Daily Check-in Streak** | Log in and claim consecutive daily bonuses | **+10 to +50 RB** |
| **High Combo Streak** | Execute 5x or higher combos in Fruit Slice | **+15 RB** |
| **Daily Quests (Tasks)** | Complete daily game objectives in the Tasks tab | **+20 to +100 RB** |

Accumulated **RB Coins** can be used in the in-game shop to unlock custom blade slicing trails, stylish background arenas, and power-up inventory boosts.
