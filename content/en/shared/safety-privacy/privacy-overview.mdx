ConnectPi is committed to protecting Pioneer privacy, ensuring transparent data governance, and upholding strict non-custodial blockchain principles across both our Web portal and Android mobile application.

---

## 1. Core Privacy Principles

ConnectPi is designed with four foundational data security pillars:

- **Non-Custodial Architecture:** ConnectPi interfaces directly with the decentralized Pi Network via official SDK protocols. Your private wallet keys and 24-word passphrase remain strictly on your own device and are never requested or stored.
- **Data Minimization:** We only collect the minimal personal and technical information required to maintain account integrity, deliver social features, and prevent fraudulent abuse.
- **User Ownership:** You retain complete control over your social content, profile details, and account lifecycle.
- **Encryption Standards:** All network transmissions are protected with TLS 1.3 encryption, and backend databases utilize AES-256 encryption at rest.

<Callout type="warning" title="Zero Passphrase Storage Guarantee">
  **ConnectPi will never ask for your 24-word Pi passphrase.**
  
  Authentication relies strictly on the official Pi Network OAuth token system. Anyone requesting your passphrase, private keys, or wallet seed phrase is an unauthorized scammer attempting to compromise your funds.
</Callout>

## 3. Content Lifecycle & Post Deletion Buffers

ConnectPi provides authors with full control over their posted content while balancing platform continuity and legal compliance:

### Immediate Public Removal
When you delete a post, comment, or media attachment, it is immediately removed from public feeds, channel streams, and user profiles.

### 20 to 30-Day Soft-Deletion Grace Period
To safeguard against accidental deletion, enable account recovery, and satisfy statutory fraud prevention and legal audit requirements, deleted posts and physical media files (photos, videos, thumbnails) are securely retained in an encrypted soft-deletion buffer for **20 to 30 days**. During this window, the content is completely inaccessible to all other users.

### Reposted & Shared Content Retention (Up to 90 Days)
If a post has been reposted, quoted, or shared by other Pioneers across ConnectPi prior to deletion, the underlying content or thread reference may be securely retained in our system for **up to 90 days** to maintain feed integrity, discussion continuity, and community context before permanent automated purging.

## 4. How to Delete Your Account

If you wish to permanently terminate your ConnectPi account and delete associated data, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Account Settings">
    Log in to ConnectPi and open **Settings** (or visit `https://connectpi.in/settings`).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Delete Account dialog">
    Scroll to the bottom of the page and select **Delete Account** (or navigate to `https://connectpi.in/delete-account`).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit your deletion request">
    Review the warning terms, optionally provide feedback regarding your departure, and tap **Submit Account Deletion Request**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="10-Day Cancellation Grace Period">
    ConnectPi provides an automatic **10-day grace period** (`DELETION_GRACE_PERIOD_DAYS = 10`):
    - If you change your mind within 10 days, simply log back in and tap **Cancel Deletion Request** to restore your account.
    - Once the 10-day window expires, your personal identifiers, profile credentials, and associated records are permanently and irrevocably purged from our production databases.
  </Step>
</Steps>
