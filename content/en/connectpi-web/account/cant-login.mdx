If you experience an infinite loading spinner, an authentication timeout, or an error when logging into ConnectPi via Pi Browser, follow this comprehensive troubleshooting guide.

---

## 1. Common Symptoms & Root Causes

| Error / Symptom | Primary Cause | Quick Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **"Pi SDK not loaded. Please use the Pi Browser."** | Opened in Chrome, Safari, or an external browser instead of Pi Browser. | Open `connectpi.in` inside Pi Browser, or use the Desktop QR flow. |
| **Connecting spinner hangs > 60 seconds** | Pi Browser WebView session cache is desynchronized or unresponsive. | Force close Pi Browser and clear temporary cache. |
| **"Authentication verification failed"** | Pi Network core API (`api.minepi.com`) timeout or expired session token. | Tap **Try Again** to request a fresh authentication grant. |
| **"Authentication was cancelled"** | The permission prompt was dismissed or **Decline** was tapped. | Click **Continue with Pi** again and tap **Allow**. |
| **Incomplete Payment dialog appears** | A previous Pi transaction was interrupted before server settlement. | Wait 5 seconds while ConnectPi auto-resolves pending transactions. |

## 2. Step-by-Step Resolution Procedures

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify your access URL inside Pi Browser">
    Ensure you are using the official Pi Browser application:
    1. Open the **Pi Browser** app on your mobile device.
    2. Enter `https://connectpi.in/signup` or `https://aditya.pinet.com/signup` in the top address bar.
    3. Ensure the protocol is `https://` to allow secure WebView cryptographic bridges.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Force close and clear Pi Browser cache">
    Corrupted WebView cache is the most frequent cause of frozen authentication dialogs.

    **On Android:**
    1. Open your phone's **Settings** > **Apps** (or **Manage Apps**).
    2. Locate and tap **Pi Browser**.
    3. Tap **Force Stop**.
    4. Go to **Storage & Cache** > tap **Clear Cache** (*do not select Clear Data/Storage*).
    5. Reopen Pi Browser and navigate back to `connectpi.in`.

    **On iOS (iPhone):**
    1. Swipe up from the bottom of your screen to open the app switcher.
    2. Swipe up on **Pi Browser** to force close it.
    3. Turn your phone's **Airplane Mode** ON for 5 seconds, then turn it back OFF to reset network sockets.
    4. Reopen Pi Browser.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the primary Pi Network mining app">
    The Pi Browser relies on the background session from your primary **Pi Network** app:
    1. Open the official **Pi Network** mining app on your phone.
    2. Ensure your account is actively logged in and your mining session is current.
    3. Tap the side menu in the Pi app > tap **Pi Browser** > select **Sign in to Pi Browser**. This refreshes the underlying cryptographic bridge between both apps.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grant required permissions in the authentication modal">
    When the **Share information with ConnectPi?** sheet appears, verify the requested scopes:
    - **Auth:** Authenticate you with your Pi account.
    - **Username:** Your verified Pi handle.
    - **Wallet address:** Your public wallet address for social tipping and marketplace transactions.
    - **In-app notifications:** Platform alerts and message updates.
    
    Tap the purple **Allow** button. If you tap **Decline**, authentication cannot proceed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use Desktop / QR code sign-in as an alternative">
    If the mobile Pi Browser WebView is experiencing persistent issues:
    1. Open `https://connectpi.in/signup` on your computer or secondary device.
    2. Select **Sign in with Pi Account** under *Desktop / Chrome / Any Browser*.
    3. Scan the generated QR code using your phone's camera to authorize securely via `accounts.pinet.com`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Critical Security Warning">
  **Never enter or share your 24-word Pi Passphrase!**
  
  ConnectPi uses official Pi Network SDK OAuth protocols. Legitimate authentication **never** asks for your passphrase, private keys, or wallet seed phrase. Anyone asking for your 24 words is an unauthorized imposter attempting to steal your funds.
</Callout>

## 3. Still Unable to Log In?

If you have completed all troubleshooting steps and still cannot authenticate:
- Check the official Pi Core Team status channels for network-wide API maintenance.
- If your account registered via Google, you can log in directly at `https://connectpi.in/login` with your username or email and password.
- Reach out to our Pioneer support team with your registered Pi username and device model.
