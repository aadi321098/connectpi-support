The native **ConnectPi Android App** delivers the fastest mobile social experience for Pioneers worldwide, featuring real-time push notifications, offline feed caching, daily reward streak reminders, and custom username creation.

---

## 1. Why Download the Android App?

While ConnectPi Web is accessible from any desktop or mobile browser, the native Android app unlocks exclusive mobile benefits:

- **Custom Username Selection:** Choose your own unique Pioneer handle (e.g. `@pioneer_alex`) during registration, rather than receiving an auto-generated username.
- **Instant Push Alerts:** Get real-time notifications when Pioneers mention you, comment on your posts, or send peer messages.
- **Daily Check-in & Quests:** Never miss your daily CP token claim with streak reminder notifications.
- **Integrated Camera & Media:** Snap photos and record videos directly inside the post editor without leaving the application.

## 2. Installation via Google Play Store

Follow these steps to install the official app safely on your Android device:

<Screenshot
  src="/images/articles/app/play-store-install.webp"
  alt="ConnectPi official app listing on Google Play Store highlighting the Install button"
  caption="The verified ConnectPi listing on Google Play Store (Package ID: in.connectpi.app)."
/>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Google Play Store listing">
    Open the official ConnectPi store page on your Android smartphone:
    
    👉 **[Download Connect Pi on Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.connectpi.app)**
    
    *(Package ID: `in.connectpi.app` | Verified Developer: Connect Pi)*
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the application">
    Tap the green **Install** button. The Play Store will verify the package signature and download the application (~30–45 MB).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch Connect Pi">
    Once installation finishes, tap **Open** or launch the **Connect Pi** app icon from your home screen or app drawer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 4. App Permissions Guide

To deliver core community features, ConnectPi requests standard Android runtime permissions. Here is why each permission is required:

| Permission | Category | Purpose in ConnectPi | Mandatory? |
|---|---|---|:---:|
| **POST_NOTIFICATIONS** | Notifications | Delivers instant alerts for daily CP check-in rewards, mentions, and incoming direct messages. | Recommended |
| **CAMERA** | Media Capture | Allows taking live photos and short videos directly within the post composer. | Optional |
| **READ_MEDIA_IMAGES** | Storage / Gallery | Required to select and upload photos or screenshots from your photo gallery. | Recommended |
| **ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION** | Location | Powers the optional **Locality Feed** and regional Pioneer marketplace discovery. | Optional |

<Callout type="tip" title="Privacy Control">
  You can manage or revoke optional permissions (such as Location or Camera) at any time from your device's **Settings > Apps > Connect Pi > Permissions**.
</Callout>

## 5. System Requirements & Specifications

- **Operating System:** Android 8.0 (API Level 26) or higher (Android 9.0+ recommended).
- **Free Storage Space:** Minimum 50 MB for app installation and media cache.
- **Internet Connectivity:** Stable 4G, 5G, or Wi-Fi connection.
- **Package Identifier:** `in.connectpi.app`
