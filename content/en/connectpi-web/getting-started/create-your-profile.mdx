Setting up your ConnectPi profile allows you to connect with fellow Pioneers, publish updates, and earn loyalty points.

## Before you start
- Ensure you have opened ConnectPi inside the **Pi Browser** or authenticated with your Pi account.
- Have an avatar image ready (JPG, PNG, or WebP).

## Step-by-step setup
<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to your Profile Settings">
    Click on the profile avatar icon in the top right header navigation. Select **Edit Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your display name and bio">
    Enter your desired display name and a brief description introducing yourself to the community.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload an avatar and save">
    Tap the **Upload Photo** button, select your image, and click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Privacy Tip">
Your Pi wallet address is never made public on your profile. Only your chosen display name and verified username are visible to other members.
</Callout>
