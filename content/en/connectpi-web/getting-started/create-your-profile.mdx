Your ConnectPi profile is your decentralized identity across the Pioneer social ecosystem. Customizing your profile helps you connect with fellow Pioneers, gain followers, and showcase your community contributions.

---

## Step-by-Step Profile Customization

Follow these steps to update your personal information and profile appearance:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to your Profile page">
    Open your browser and visit:
    [connectpi.in/profile](https://connectpi.in/profile)

    Alternatively, you can click on **Profile** under the **MY SPACE** section in the left navigation sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the 'Edit Profile' button">
    On your main profile overview, locate the **Edit Profile** button positioned directly below your cover banner, adjacent to your username handle.

    <Screenshot
      src="/images/articles/web/profile-overview-edit-btn.webp"
      alt="ConnectPi user profile header displaying the Edit Profile button"
      caption="Click 'Edit Profile' on your profile dashboard to open the settings modal."
    />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Update your basic information">
    The **Edit Profile** modal will open with the following fields:
    - **Avatar Photo:** Click the camera icon overlaid on your profile picture to upload a new avatar (JPG, PNG, or WebP).
    - **Name \*:** Enter your preferred display name. This is the name shown in bold across all your posts, replies, and community feeds.
    - **Email:** Provide your contact email address (kept private and used for account notifications).
    - **Bio:** Write a brief introduction about yourself, your Pioneer journey, or your community interests.
    - **Links (comma-separated):** Add external websites, social profiles, or community channels (separate multiple URLs with commas).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Once you have updated your information, click the blue **Save Changes** button in the bottom right corner of the dialog. Your profile will refresh and update immediately!

    <Screenshot
      src="/images/articles/web/edit-profile-modal-fields.webp"
      alt="Edit Profile modal dialog showing Name, Email, Bio, Links fields and Save Changes button"
      caption="Review your updated details and click 'Save Changes' to apply your new profile information."
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

<Callout type="note" title="Notice Regarding Custom Usernames">
  Custom username modification is currently **not available** on the Web platform. Your username (handle) is permanently linked to your verified registration identifier (such as your Pi Network UID or auto-assigned Google handle).

  A dedicated feature allowing Pioneers to customize or upgrade their username (through future free or premium plan updates) is currently under development.
</Callout>

## Exploring Profile Features

Your ConnectPi profile includes several tools to manage your presence and rewards:

* **View Card:** Explore your personalized **Profile Card**—a shareable digital badge with custom links, QR code, and subscription options.
* **Analytics:** Track impressions, post reach, follower growth, and community engagement metrics.
* **Claim Pi & Loyalty Points:** Access your daily check-in rewards and view your loyalty points balance.
* **Posts & Bookmarks Tabs:** Easily filter between your published community posts and articles you have bookmarked for later reading.

<Callout type="tip" title="Privacy Protection">
  ConnectPi adheres to strict privacy principles. Your sensitive account details—including your private Pi wallet keys and internal credentials—are never exposed publicly on your profile page.
</Callout>
