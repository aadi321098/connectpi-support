Get real-time push notifications, faster messaging, and instant updates with the native ConnectPi mobile application.

## System requirements
- Android 9.0 (Pie) or higher.
- Minimum 50 MB free storage space.

## Installation steps
<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the APK or open Google Play Store">
    Download the official package from [PLACEHOLDER: Google Play Store link or Direct APK link].
  </Step>
  <Step title="Permit installation if sideloading">
    If installing an APK directly, enable **Allow from this source** in your Android security settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch and connect">
    Open the app and tap **Continue with Pi Network** to pair your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Note">
Notification permissions are requested on first launch so you do not miss critical loyalty point rewards or direct messages.
</Callout>
