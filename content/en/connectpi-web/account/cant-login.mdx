If you see an authentication timeout or an infinite loading spinner when logging into ConnectPi, follow these quick troubleshooting steps.

## Quick checklist
- Make sure you are accessing `connectpi.in` directly within the official **Pi Browser** app.
- Check that your Pi Network account KYC status is active.

## Resolution steps
<Steps>
  <Step title="Restart Pi Browser">
    Force-close the Pi Browser app from your mobile task manager and launch it again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear temporary browser cache">
    In your phone settings, go to **Apps** > **Pi Browser** > **Storage** and select **Clear Cache**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry Pi authentication">
    Open `connectpi.in` and tap **Sign in with Pi**. Accept the permissions prompt when presented.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Warning">
Never share your 24-word Pi passphrase with anyone. ConnectPi will never ask for your passphrase or private keys.
</Callout>
