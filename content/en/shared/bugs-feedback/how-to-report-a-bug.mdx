Found a glitch or an unexpected behavior? Reporting clear steps helps our engineering team deploy rapid bug fixes.

## What to include in a bug report
1. **Device & OS:** What phone or browser (e.g. Android 13, Pi Browser v1.6.1, Chrome 122).
2. **Steps to reproduce:** What action you performed immediately prior to the error.
3. **Expected vs Actual behavior:** What should have happened compared to what actually happened.
