Encountering an issue or have an idea to make ConnectPi better? Detailed bug reports and constructive feedback allow our engineering team to rapidly diagnose issues, release software patches, and prioritize community requests.

---

## 1. Official Support Channels

You can submit technical bug reports and product feedback through either of our primary channels:

- **Web Support Portal (Recommended):** Submit an inquiry directly via our [Contact Support Form](/contact). This automatically captures your device and browser diagnostics.
- **Direct Support Email:** Contact our engineering team at [contact@connectpi.in](mailto:contact@connectpi.in).

---

## 2. Choosing the Affected Product

When filing a report, please specify which platform was affected:

| Platform | Product Slug | Focus Areas |
|---|---|---|
| **ConnectPi Web** | `connectpi-web` | Desktop / mobile browser social feeds (`connectpi.in`), Pi Browser login, post creation, profile settings. |
| **ConnectPi Android App** | `connectpi-app` | Native Android APK (`in.connectpi.app`), push notifications, custom username picker, camera/media upload. |
| **Rush Box** | `rushbox` | Web arcade (`rushbox.in`) and Play Store release (`com.connectpi.bladerush`), Fruit Slice combos, RB coins balance, game lag. |
| **Account & Safety** | `shared` | Security concerns, privacy requests, terms of service, or general community questions. |

---

## 3. Anatomy of an Actionable Bug Report

To help our developers reproduce and resolve issues quickly, please structure your report using this checklist:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Provide clear environment details">
    Specify your device model, operating system, and browser or app version:
    - *Example:* Samsung Galaxy S23, Android 14, Pi Browser v1.6.1.
    - *Example:* Windows 11 PC, Google Chrome v124, Web desktop feed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Document step-by-step reproduction steps">
    List the exact actions that lead to the glitch:
    1. Opened `connectpi.in/signup` inside Pi Browser.
    2. Tapped **Continue with Pi**.
    3. The authentication spinner spun for 60 seconds without displaying the permission modal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Contrast expected vs actual behavior">
    - **Expected:** The Pi permission grant modal should appear within 3 seconds.
    - **Actual:** The spinner froze indefinitely until displaying an authentication timeout error.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Attach relevant screenshots or screen recordings">
    Attach clear screenshots showing the error prompt, console warning, or visual glitch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

<Callout type="warning" title="Privacy & Security Rule">
  **Never include passwords, private wallet keys, or 24-word Pi passphrases in bug reports or screenshots.**
  
  Please blur or crop any confidential personal credentials before uploading attachments to support tickets.
</Callout>

## 4. Submitting Feature Feedback & Community Ideas

We love hearing ideas from Pioneers! If you want to suggest a new feature (e.g. new mini-game for Rush Box, localized community channels, or feed discovery filters):

1. Select **Feedback & Suggestions** on our [Contact Form](/contact).
2. Clearly explain the proposed feature and why it would benefit the broader Pioneer community.
3. Suggest practical UX workflows or references where applicable.

Community feedback is reviewed weekly during product roadmap planning sessions.
