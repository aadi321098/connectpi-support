In addition to Pi Network authentication, ConnectPi Web allows Pioneers to create an account using **Google Authentication** and log in securely with their email/username and password.

---

## Part 1: How to Sign Up with Google

Follow these steps to register your ConnectPi account using your Google profile:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Google Sign-Up page">
    Open your browser and navigate to:
    [connectpi.in/signup-google](https://www.connectpi.in/signup-google)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Accept the terms and age confirmation">
    Before proceeding, you must check both required confirmation boxes:
    1. **Terms & Policies:** Agree to ConnectPi's *Terms of Service*, *Privacy Policy*, and *Refund Policy*.
    2. **Age of Majority:** Confirm that you have reached the legal age of majority in your jurisdiction (at least 18 years old).

    Both checkboxes must be selected for the authentication button to activate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click 'Authenticate with Google'">
    Click the blue **Authenticate with Google** button and choose your preferred Google account from the standard Google Sign-In prompt.

    <Screenshot
      src="/images/articles/web/google-signup-consent.webp"
      alt="ConnectPi Sign Up with Google page highlighting the terms checkboxes and authenticate button"
      caption="Accept both terms checkboxes and click 'Authenticate with Google' to proceed."
    />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review your email and generated username">
    Once authenticated, ConnectPi verifies your email address as your unique identifier and provides an auto-generated username.

    <Screenshot
      src="/images/articles/web/google-signup-details-redacted.webp"
      alt="Confirm your details screen showing verified email, generated username, and Continue button"
      caption="Verify your details and click 'Continue' to complete your profile setup."
    />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete profile details and set your password">
    Click the **Continue** button. You will be prompted to enter:
    - **Display Name:** Your community name visible on posts and comments.
    - **Bio:** A brief introduction about yourself.
    - **Avatar:** An optional profile picture.
    - **Password:** Choose a secure password for your account.

    Submit the form to finalize your account registration!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Want to choose a custom username? Use the ConnectPi Mobile App">
  On the Web version, Google sign-up assigns an auto-generated username. If you want to select a **custom username** during registration, download the official **ConnectPi Mobile App** for Android:
  
  👉 [Download ConnectPi on Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.connectpi.app) *(Package ID: `in.connectpi.app`)*
</Callout>

## Part 2: How to Log In with Your Credentials

Once your account has been registered, you can log in directly at any time using your username/email and password:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ConnectPi Login page">
    Go to:
    [connectpi.in/login](https://www.connectpi.in/login)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    - In the **Username or UID** field, type your registered username, email address, or UID.
    - In the **Password** field, type your account password.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click 'Login'">
    Click the blue **Login** button. You will be authenticated immediately and taken to your personal ConnectPi home feed.

    <Screenshot
      src="/images/articles/web/connectpi-login-credentials.webp"
      alt="ConnectPi login page highlighting username, password fields, and the Login button"
      caption="Enter your registered username/email and password, then click 'Login'."
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>
