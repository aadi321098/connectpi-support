Blade Rush is an arcade fruit-slicing minigame integrated directly into the ConnectPi ecosystem where high scores convert into loyalty reward tokens.

## Basic rules
- Swipe across fruits to slice them before they drop off screen.
- Avoid slicing red hazardous bombs.
- Chain 3 or more fruits in a single swipe to trigger a **Combo Multiplier**.

## Controls
- **Desktop:** Click and drag mouse cursor rapidly across flying targets.
- **Mobile / Touchscreen:** Swipe your finger across screen.

<Callout type="tip" title="Bonus Tip">
Slicing golden dragonfruits grants an instant 10-second frenzy mode where bomb spawns are paused!
</Callout>
